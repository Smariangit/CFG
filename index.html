ROUND 01

The Garage

CFG didn't start in a glass-front studio. It started in an actual garage — heavy bags hung from ceiling joists, a mismatched set of dumbbells, and a coach who refused to make it comfortable.

That's still the standard. We kept the name, the concrete floor, and the philosophy: fitness that's tested under pressure, not performed in a mirror. Every class ends the way a round does — when the work is actually done.

/01

Fighter-first coaching

Every coach on the mat has competed. They teach what held up when it mattered.

/02

Small classes, real correction

Capped headcounts so your footwork gets fixed, not ignored.

/03

Built for progress, not vibes

Sparring logs, strength benchmarks, and a visible path from white-flag to ring-ready.
